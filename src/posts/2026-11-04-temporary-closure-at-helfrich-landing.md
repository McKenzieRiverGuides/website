November 13 & 16–20, 2026

Tree work is scheduled at the Vida McKenzie Community Center during the week of November 13 and 16–20.

Brinks will be completing tree work on the property, and the Helfrich Landing boat ramp and parking lot will be temporarily closed during this time. The closure will provide the tree crews with adequate space to safely bring down the trees.

We encourage local boaters, guides, outfitters, and community members to plan accordingly and make alternative arrangements during this period.

Please help spread the word to anyone who may be planning to use the ramp. Thank you for your patience and understanding as this work is completed!
