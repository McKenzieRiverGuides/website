The current has created an opening in the log jam below Bellinger Landing at the bottom of the powerline flat. If you decide to run this route, please be cautious and stay to the left.

Additionally, the obstruction at Fish Rock has been cut on both ends and is likely to wash out with the next high water. Avoid launching at Olallie until the log is confirmed to be clear.

Aside from these two updates, I’m pleased to report that there are no major obstructions on the McKenzie River. Stay safe out there!
